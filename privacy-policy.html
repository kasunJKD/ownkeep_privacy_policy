Privacy Policy for OwnKeep

Last updated: 9 September 2026

OwnKeep is an offline-first app for keeping the records of things you own — warranties, receipts, serial numbers, documents, service history, payments and reminders. Your library is stored on your device. We do not run an account system, and we do not collect, sell or share your ownership information.

1. Who we are

This app ("OwnKeep", "the app", package com.ownkeep.app) is published by the developer listed on its Google Play store page ("we", "us"). You can reach us at the email address in the Contact section below.

2. Data you enter stays on your device

Everything you add to OwnKeep is written to storage that is private to the app on your device:

  • Items and their details — name, brand, model, category, serial and barcode numbers, location/room, condition and notes.
  • Purchase details — dates, prices, currency, where you bought it.
  • Receipts, warranties, insurance policies, subscriptions and memberships, including amounts and renewal dates you type in.
  • Photos and files you attach (receipt images, item photos, manuals, PDFs). When you attach a file, OwnKeep copies it into its own private storage; that copy remains even if you later delete the original.
  • Service and repair history, reminders, custom categories and payment types.
  • App settings and preferences (theme, currency, onboarding state).

This data is not transmitted to us and is not stored on any server we control. It is not backed up to us. It stays on the device until you delete it, uninstall the app, or export it yourself.

Note: if you have Android's system backup enabled for the app, Android may include the app's data in your own Google account backup. That is controlled by your device settings, not by OwnKeep, and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of OwnKeep shows a single banner ad, served by Google AdMob. To do this, the Google Mobile Ads SDK included in the app may collect and process:

  • Device and app identifiers (including the Android Advertising ID).
  • Approximate (coarse) location derived from your IP address.
  • Ad interaction and diagnostic data (ad requests, impressions, clicks, crashes, performance).

This information is collected by Google as an independent controller and is used to serve and measure ads and to limit how often you see the same ad. We do not receive your receipts, item data or personal library from AdMob, and your OwnKeep library is never used for ad targeting.

For more on how Google uses this data, see:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • AdMob & AdSense privacy and messaging

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in a region that requires it, OwnKeep uses Google's consent management (the UMP SDK) to ask, on first launch, whether you agree to personalised ads. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time from the consent option in the app. Where you decline, ads are limited to non-personalised ads, which still require basic requests to Google's ad servers.

Removing ads

You can remove ads by purchasing the ad-free / Premium option from within the app (Settings → Remove ads). Purchases are processed by Google Play; we do not receive your payment card details. When ads are removed, the ads SDK no longer requests ads.

5. Exporting and sharing — your choice

OwnKeep can generate a CSV of your library or a PDF record for a single item (for insurance claims, resale or warranty service). These files are created on your device and are only shared if you send them somewhere using Android's share sheet. We are not involved in and do not receive those files.

6. Cloud sync

The current release does not upload your library to any OwnKeep server. Any "Cloud Sync" area shown in the app is not yet active. If we introduce an optional cloud backup or multi-device sync in a future version, it will be off by default, and we will update this policy to describe what is stored and where before that feature is enabled.

7. Children

OwnKeep is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Delete everything: Settings → Delete local data erases all items, attachments and settings from the device. Uninstalling the app also removes its data.
  • Reset your advertising identifier or opt out of ad personalisation in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Withdraw ad consent (EEA/UK/CH) from the consent option in the app.
  • Because your library never reaches us, requests to access, correct or delete that data are fulfilled entirely on your device using the controls above. For data held by Google as described in section 3, contact Google or use your Google account controls.

9. Data retention and security

We do not hold your library, so we have nothing to retain or delete on our side. On your device, data is kept in app-private storage that other apps cannot read. No method of electronic storage is completely secure; keeping your device locked and up to date is the best protection for the information in OwnKeep.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the current version, and significant changes will be reflected in the app's store listing.
